Insurance language is built to be confusing. Here are the four words that matter, in order.

## Deductible

What you pay first each year before the plan starts paying its share. If your deductible is $1,500, you pay the first $1,500 of covered care, then the plan starts helping.

## Copay or coinsurance

After the deductible, you pay a piece of each visit. A **copay** is a flat amount, like $30. **Coinsurance** is a percentage, like 20% of the visit.

## Out-of-pocket max

The most you can pay in a year. After you hit it, the plan pays 100% of covered care. For families doing many hours of therapy a week, this is the number that matters most, because you usually reach it.

## Authorization

The plan's approval for therapy hours. We request it for you. You never have to argue with your insurer about hours.

## What Texas law says

Texas requires most state-regulated health plans to cover ABA therapy for a child diagnosed with autism before their 10th birthday. Self-funded employer plans follow federal rules instead. We will tell you which kind you have. Texas Medicaid has also covered ABA for eligible children since 2022.
