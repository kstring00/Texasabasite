Parents ask about the first day more than anything else. Here is what it looks like, so there are no surprises.

## Before you arrive

Your child's BCBA (the Board Certified Behavior Analyst who wrote the plan with you) has already met your child during the evaluation or the tour. Your child's RBT (Registered Behavior Technician, the person who will be with them in the room) has read the plan and knows what your child loves.

Bring a comfort item, a snack, and for little ones a change of clothes. That is it.

## The first hour

The first day is shorter than a normal session on purpose. The goal is not to learn anything. The goal is for your child to decide the room is safe and the RBT is fun.

You can stay. Many parents sit in the parent area where they can see in. Some sit in the room. Either is fine. Tell us what you want.

## What "play-based" means on day one

Your child picks. The RBT follows. If your child wants the trains, the session is about trains. The skills in the plan, like asking for more, taking a turn, or coming when called, get folded into what your child already wants to do.

## When you leave

Before you go, you and the BCBA agree on how to talk during the week. Text, a quick call, or a chat at pickup. You pick.

## The next few days

Sessions get a little longer as your child settles in. Within the first couple of weeks you will have your first parent training session, where you learn what the RBT does so it works at home too.

If you want to see the room before day one, [request a tour](/request-a-tour). It is the easiest way to take the mystery out of it.
