The pediatrician said the word. Now there is a gap, and the gap is the hardest part. Three things families tell us helped.

## 1. Write down what you have noticed

Not a diagnosis. Just a list. When did you first wonder. What does your child do that made you wonder. What do they love. What is hard. Bring it to the evaluation. It makes the visit faster and the report truer.

## 2. Talk to one parent who has been here

You do not need a support group yet. You need one person a few steps ahead of you. In [Common Ground](/families), our parent space, there are circles forming for exactly this stage. You can join before your child is enrolled anywhere.

## 3. Ask about the money early

The sooner we know your plan, the sooner you know what the evaluation and therapy would cost. We check your benefits for free, and knowing the number takes a weight off. [Ask for a benefits check](/request-a-tour?about=coverage).

## One more thing

An evaluation does not commit you to anything. It gives you an answer. If the answer is autism, we can start planning care the same week. If it is not, you leave with a clear picture and a plan for what to watch. Either way, you are not waiting in the dark.

If you want to see a center first, [request a tour](/request-a-tour).
