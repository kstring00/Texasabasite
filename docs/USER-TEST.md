# 5-minute user test

Run this with one person who has never seen the site. A phone, not a laptop. Do not help. Write down every pause.

## The script (read aloud)

> You're a parent in Pearland. Yesterday your pediatrician mentioned the word "autism" about your three-year-old. You've opened this website for the first time. Figure out three things: Is this the right place for your child? What will it cost you? And when you're ready, request a tour. Think out loud the whole time.

Start a timer. Stop at 5 minutes whether or not they finish.

## What you are watching for

| Minute | What should happen | Write down if it doesn't |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | They can say what the site is, who it's for, and where, without scrolling. | The words they use. If they say "therapy place" but not "Texas" or "evaluation," the first screen is not doing its job. |
| 0:10–0:45 | They type a ZIP or "Pearland" into the finder, or scroll to the centers and find Pearland. | Did they see the finder? Did they trust the result? |
| 0:45–2:00 | They answer "is this right for my child" using: the 5-step path, the diagnosis card ("we do the evaluation in-house"), Early Intervention (age 2+), or the "what ABA is" line. | Which section they used. Any section they skipped. The moment they said "I'm not sure this is for us." |
| 2:00–3:30 | They find the cost answer: "free benefits check," the Texas law line, or the plain-English terms. | Did they expect a price and feel let down? Did "we verify for free" feel like an answer or a dodge? |
| 3:30–5:00 | They tap Request a tour (sticky bar, hero, or Pearland card), fill four fields, submit, and read the confirmation. | Every field they hesitated on. Whether they opened the optional section. Whether they read "While you wait" on the confirmation or closed the tab. |

## Questions after the timer

1. What is the first thing that would happen if you submitted this?
2. Who would contact you? (Looking for: a named person, by text, within a business day.)
3. Does your child need a diagnosis first? Where did you learn that?
4. What would it cost you? What's the next step to find out?
5. What is Common Ground? (Looking for: something for parents, free, available before you start.)
6. What did you want to know that you couldn't find?
7. Did anything feel like marketing?

## Known gaps a tester will probably hit (fix before launch)

- No real center photos yet; the illustrated placeholders say so. Testers may ask "what does it look like inside."
- No insurance logos yet. Testers with Aetna or BCBS will want to see their plan name.
- No clinical lead name on the Pearland page yet. "Who will be with my child" is answered generically (BCBA / RBT), not by name.
- Parent circles are marked "pilot forming." A tester may read that as "not real yet." That is honest; decide whether to show it before the circles are meeting.
- The tour form does not pre-fill the center from the finder result unless they tap the Pearland "Request a tour" button. If they use the header button they must choose the center from the list.
